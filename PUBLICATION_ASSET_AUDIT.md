# Publication asset audit

Scope: current tracked assets and the duplicate locations removed/relocated from
baseline `2eb40fd44b48baa31b31dcd1047ab89f8a128c3b`. File contents, SDF/URDF/Mapviz
references, image representations and DAE metadata were inspected. This is not a
legal clearance or a claim that all source rights have been established.

The manuscript publicly identifies CTVI. Site coordinates alone are therefore
**not classified as confidential merely because they identify that site**. Imagery
and CAD redistribution rights are a separate question. No evidence here establishes
that Bing, Google or another imagery provider granted redistribution permission.
No partner asset was deleted just because of a location or brand name.

remain, byte-for-byte. Recommendations are owner-review actions, not actions

## Owner actions and scope limits

1. Confirm original suppliers and permissions for all aerial imagery, heightmaps
   and CAD/mesh geometry, including partner-derived modifications.
2. Record any license/permission evidence alongside the appropriate assets. If
   permission is unavailable, select a replacement/removal in a separately
   reviewed change; doing so may affect the scientific model or visualization.
3. Keep coordinate/site identification separate from image ownership. CTVI's
   appearance in the manuscript does not by itself license a satellite image.
4. Review exact routes and vehicle engineering details against actual partner
   agreements. None were supplied for this audit.
5. Raw recordings are distributed through the supplied Zenodo DOI, whose record
   terms and upload completeness were not verified. No recording was moved into Git.

No dataset, world definition, unique model or image was deleted. Duplicate paths
were removed only after confirming that their content survived in the canonical
location. The exposed Bing key remains a history-remediation issue, detailed in
`docs/SECURITY_AUDIT.md`.
